Recommendation

Use C with the five-point safety margin if one-sided 5% inference is acceptable. Treat 24×2 as exploratory under these try-noise assumptions. At roughly 48 tries per arm, 24×2 offers broader case coverage; 16×3 needs fewer factory cases. Prefer a fixed sample: the validated interim plan saves less than one case on average even with no benefit. An 80% confirmatory goal needs a larger pre-fixed cap or credible evidence of lower repeat noise.

The first eight cases cannot certify that 24 is enough from case correlation alone. Measure paired differences and repeat noise together; do not enlarge the scored study after viewing results without a pre-fixed adaptation.
