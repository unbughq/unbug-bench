# Fresh-batch all-outcomes integration

Implementation and qualification are in progress. No full-suite or diff-review
pass is claimed yet.

The new `tests/factory/test_stream_d_all_outcomes.py` joins real installed batch
policy, ordinary capture, infrastructure exhaustion, accounting finalization,
restore authentication, diagnosis dispatch, writer admission and package outcome
production. One test method runs named branches on isolated fresh-case fixtures.
No production function, allowance, gate version or snapshot schema is changed.

The initial exhaustion follows the parent's decision after design round 2:
two authored G5 child-start errors pass through real `gate_attempt` and
`stop_infrastructure`. `run_gates` returns with `c.stopped`, G5 is `invalid-infra`,
and the first entry retains the stress/G1–G4 prefix with G5 unreached. No budget
predicate is replaced to manufacture an exhaustion.

The exam covers partial entry, publication-ACK recovery, progressing extensions,
completed-package replay, unchanged/irrelevant edits, G1/early-G5/saved-cheat
rejection, public match, blocking/request-only stress, ordinary and post-extension
factory-bug returns, three-resume and five-extension caps, not-converging and
malformed diagnosis, deadline retirement, and disabled batch policy. It asserts
the exact seven current-schema `retired_*` labels. Missing scored proof, corrupt
support and interrupted freshness mutation are explicit negative branches.

The model CLI, router lease transport, initial ordinary stress response, UID/pilot
operations, checkpoint encryption/upload and comparison IPC are authored
boundaries. Real G4/G5 consume saved plain/reference executions on the suffix-case
contract. Real G1 consumes the saved practice contract and copied bench project.
Public-match confirmation uses retained HTTP bytes. Other gate answers are
labelled controls; no real worker, model or network call is launched.

Each resume serializes a new checkpoint card, imports its ledger/scorecard through
`restore_ledger.install`, delivers and installs the hash-bound job config, inherits
recovery history and selects models through the runner functions. Cross-batch
money histories and factory-bug admissions are produced by the real installers
and diagnosis code, never seeded. Retirement guards forbid writer admission and
evaluation; replay checks proof/decision bytes, reservations and diagnosis state.

## Limits of the evidence

This is an in-process route integration exam. It does not qualify main/VM boot,
archive encryption, Linux process containment or a real model. Murphy 9/13 and
`new-stream-d-current-comparison-1` remain open; no row closes here. The latter's
remaining new-worker/archive-restore qualification belongs to the founder's
three-case pilot.

Permanently absent or corrupt proof remains a safe hold, not an automatic
reconstruction. Retired/pre-schema adapters are excluded by the founder's scope.
The irrelevant-edit branch proves no progress under fixed gate outcomes, not a
general guarantee against stochastic rerolls of non-reusable gates. Batch gate
money pressure waits with `money_wait`; it does not trigger diagnosis.
