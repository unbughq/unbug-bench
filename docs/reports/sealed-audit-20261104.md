- **Hidden: 50.** Every `sealed/case-*.scorecard.json` card except one.
- **Excluded: 1.** `case-20261014T130816Z-a869de` is a dry run
  (`source: fixture (dry-run)`).

    `tests/factory/fixtures/` and on the practice branches, and the inventory
    never reads them.
  - Running the tool with `--cards` pointed at the real smoke card
    (`docs/reports/provisioning-startup-smoke/local-startup/`) reports that
    card as excluded.
- **Public-eligible: 0.** No hidden card has a G16 or G17 pass,
  `owner_contract` or `delivery_provenance`. The existing admission check
  `verify_exam_metadata` therefore rejects every card:
  - 47 cards fail with "audit requires a verified sealed case" (G1-G15 not all
    passed);
  - 3 cards fail with "exam requires the same completed fairness checks for
    every case" (G16/G17 absent).
- **Qualified: 0.** Two of the eight criteria always need evidence that public
  files cannot give:
  - the archive-backed `verify_exam`;
  - an attestation that no contestant developer saw plaintext.

  Public evidence can therefore never qualify a case on its own. Here it does
  not even make one eligible.

  audit rerun `sealed_audit.run` produces neither, so this is an open
